02 / Case study

Sephora · Summer 2026

MiRA

Voice · Vision · Reasoning

Multimodal Intelligence and Reasoning Architecture

From speech-model evaluation to a production multimodal service layer.

Scope of ownership

System architecture, model integration, retrieval, deployment, and production troubleshooting.

The mandate

I joined Sephora’s AI Beauty Chat team in summer 2026 with a focused brief: evaluate speech-to-text models. The product serves 10+ million users, and the team had won the Grand Prize in the LVMH Business of Tomorrow Cup. I expanded that initial exploration into MiRA, a service layer that brings voice interaction, visual search, and spoken review highlights to the same product experience.

The system

I built real-time voice infrastructure over WebSockets, integrating gpt-4o-transcribe, gpt-4o-mini-tts, and streaming speech recognition through a realtime model gateway. LangGraph and LiteLLM supported orchestration and model routing. For visual retrieval, I fine-tuned CLIP with LoRA and indexed more than 12,000 products in PostgreSQL with pgvector, grounding image-based search in the product catalog.

Production ownership

I carried the system through Azure and Kubernetes deployment, with stateless microservices, KEDA autoscaling, CI/CD through GitHub Actions and Opsera, and a feedback loop for model improvement. Operational work included diagnosing autoscaling, gateway, and model-routing failures. I also presented MiRA four times: an internal showcase for 100+ people, a dedicated review with an SVP, VP, and Senior Directors, an internal tech sync, and the intern program’s closing showcase.

Outcome

Return offer to rejoin Sephora as a GenAI Scientist in January 2027.

05 / Perspective

A model is one part of the system.

Building MiRA made the relationship between model quality and system quality concrete. A useful model still depends on retrieval, latency, deployment, failure handling, and feedback. Owning the complete path changed how I evaluate an AI system: by the experience it can sustain in production.

Working through graduate school far from family has shaped that approach. I value consistent execution, clear communication, and taking responsibility for the details. I also want to keep showing up for the people in my life while doing work I’m proud of.
